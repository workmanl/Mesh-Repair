Privacy policy

MeshRepair, published by Emrld Labs.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary

MeshRepair runs locally on your Mac and never uploads your 3D files to any server. Emrld Labs collects no personal data, includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and requires no user account.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app.

What we collect

None. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

  • Number of free exports used (stored in local UserDefaults)
  • Pro Unlock purchase status managed by Apple StoreKit
  • STL files that you explicitly choose to save through the macOS Save dialog

Network use

MeshRepair contains no URLSession networking code for core features and works offline. Network access is used only by Apple StoreKit when purchasing or restoring Pro Unlock, or when you click external support and privacy links in the Help window.

Purchases

Pro Unlock is processed by Apple through the Mac App Store. Emrld Labs never receives or stores your payment details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
